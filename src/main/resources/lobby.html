Full Steam — Game Guide & How to Play

Overview

Full Steam is a fast-paced, top-down 2D multiplayer tactical shooter powered by real-time physics and WebSockets. Battle human opponents and AI bots across diverse game modes, design fully customized 100-point weapon loadouts, and adapt to environmental hazards!

How to Play (Create or Join)

  • Creating a Game: Click any preset under Create New Game (e.g. Free For All, CTF, KOTH, Headquarters). Click the ⚙️ gear icon on a preset to tweak its rules, or click ⚙️ Custom Game to build a match from scratch.
  • Joining a Game: Browse Join Active Games in the lobby list. Click 🎯 Join Game to deploy into combat immediately.
  • Loadout: After creating or joining a game, you will customize your loadout: selecting your primary weapon attributes, utility weapon, and name.
  • Spectating: Click 👁️ Spectate to observe active matches with spectator controls without occupying a player slot.
